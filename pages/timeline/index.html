Timeline

Projects tell the story better than dates alone.

Learning Never Happened In Isolation

This timeline is not a list of milestones. It is a record of how one project naturally led to another. Every repository introduced a new concept, and those ideas later reappeared in larger and more ambitious work.

2022 · Small Experiments

I started by writing small utilities and language experiments. They were compact projects, but they taught me how to automate repetitive tasks, explore unfamiliar ideas, and finish software from start to finish.

  • Scripts and Small Programs
  • Brainfuck Interpreter
  • Expression Evaluator
  • Brute Force Hello World

2023 · Discovering Kotlin & Linux

My focus shifted toward Kotlin and the Linux desktop. I explored DSLs, desktop customization, and began building applications instead of isolated utilities.

  • Tic Tac Toe with Kotlin DSL
  • Panel Transparency for Plasma
  • Tanoshi Multiplatform (began)

2024 · Building Larger Systems

Projects became more architectural. I spent more time understanding frameworks, desktop environments, packaging, and open source workflows than simply writing new features.

  • Tanoshi Multiplatform continued
  • Deflate Jar
  • Open source contributions

2025 · Understanding Algorithms

Compression algorithms became an opportunity to combine theory with implementation. Instead of using existing libraries, I wanted to understand how the underlying algorithms actually worked.

  • Huffman Encoding

2026 · Refinement

Recent work has focused on improving my development environment and experimenting with lower-level ideas. Rather than chasing entirely new technologies, I have been refining the tools I use every day.

  • Dotfiles
  • Ballooning Obfuscation
  • This portfolio

Recurring Themes

  • Curiosity before complexity.
  • Build first, optimize later.
  • Read source code to understand systems.
  • Algorithms are tools, not goals.
  • Developer experience matters.

What's Next

I expect the next chapter to continue exploring Linux, developer tooling, systems programming, and open source. The timeline will keep growing, but the motivation remains the same: learn by building.
