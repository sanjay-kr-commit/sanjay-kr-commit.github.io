Welcome

Welcome to my corner of the internet. This portfolio isn't meant to be a gallery of polished screenshots or a list of technologies. It's a collection of projects, experiments, notes, and ideas that have shaped how I think about software.

I enjoy building software, but I enjoy understanding software even more. Many of my repositories started with a simple question: "How does this actually work?" The answer usually turned into a weekend experiment, which later became a project worth documenting.

What You'll Find

This website is organized into two parts.

  • Pages describe how I think, work, and learn.
  • Projects document individual engineering efforts from motivation to implementation.

You'll find work spanning Kotlin Multiplatform, Linux desktop customization, developer tooling, compression algorithms, parser experiments, bytecode manipulation, and open source contributions.

A Small Philosophy

"Curiosity is usually the first commit."

I rarely begin with a product idea. More often, I begin with something I don't fully understand. Implementing a compression algorithm, writing a Brainfuck interpreter, or customizing a desktop environment are all different expressions of the same habit: learning by building.
